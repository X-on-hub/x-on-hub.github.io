Stop doing the same combo wrong a hundred times. This section lets you break down combos into steps. For each combo, you can:

We all have that one matchup that feels impossible. Use this space to write down what works and what doesn't against specific characters. It is easy to autopilot for an hour and get nothing done. This built-in timer helps you structure your practice. Set a session length (like 20 minutes)) and the toolkit will help you stay on task.. ### 📈 Ranked Tracking
Input your results after each ranked set.. The toolkit automatically builds a graph of your progress over time.. You will see trends: are you winning more after a specific training session? Are you slipping on certain days? This data takes the guesswork out of whether your training is actually working..

### 🔍 Replay Analytics
Modern fighting games are about adaptation... The toolkit includes a simple log section for your replays.
